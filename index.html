Privacy Policy

Sanctuary Discord Bots

This Privacy Policy describes how Sanctuary Discord bots operate within the Sanctuary Diablo communities, including the Diablo IV and Diablo II: Resurrected Discord servers partnered with Blizzard Entertainment. These bots support moderation, community management, event tracking, and server functionality.

Sanctuary Support — Data Usage

Sanctuary Support processes user messages submitted directly to the bot for moderation and support purposes.

Information that may be processed

  • Direct messages sent to the bot
  • Ticket conversations between moderators and users
  • Server membership and role verification
  • Moderation status checks, such as bans or restrictions
  • Support history required to resolve tickets

How this information is used

This information is used strictly for moderation, support operations, appeals, reports, disputes, rule clarification, bug reports, exploit escalation, and community safety.

Data retention

Support-related data may be temporarily stored for moderation continuity and ticket resolution purposes. Support data is retained only as reasonably necessary for moderation workflows.

  • Sanctuary Support does not use data for advertising
  • Sanctuary Support does not profile users
  • Sanctuary Support does not sell user information
  • Sanctuary Support does not use data for AI or machine learning training

Sanctuary Wanderer — User Data

Sanctuary Wanderer does not collect, store, sell, profile, or retain personal user data outside of Discord.

Functionality provided

  • Diablo event tracking, including World Bosses, Helltides, Legions, and similar events
  • Blizzard update notifications
  • Role assignment and automation
  • Community utilities and slash commands
  • Thread and channel organization
  • Moderation assistance features

Data retention

Sanctuary Wanderer does not maintain a database of user messages, user activity history, presence information, or behavioral profiles.

  • Does not store message content
  • Does not store user activity history
  • Does not track user presence
  • Does not build user profiles
  • Does not use data for analytics or advertising
  • Does not sell user information
  • Does not use data for AI or machine learning training
Any Discord data accessed by Sanctuary Wanderer is used solely to provide requested functionality and is not retained beyond operational requirements necessary to execute commands or moderation assistance actions.

Third-Party Services

The bots may access approved third-party services and APIs to provide functionality, including Diablo-related event information, Blizzard updates, Ko-Fi supporter verification, and server integrations.

These services are used solely to provide community features.

Data Requests

Users who have questions regarding moderation-related data or wish to request deletion of support-related information may contact the Sanctuary moderation team through Discord or by opening a support request.

Because Sanctuary Wanderer does not retain user data off-platform, there may be no persistent user data available for deletion.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect operational data used for moderation and support purposes. Access to support-related information is limited to authorized moderation staff where needed for community operations.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect operational, legal, or platform-related changes.

Continued use of Sanctuary Discord services constitutes acceptance of any updates to this policy.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, moderation practices, or bot functionality, please contact the Sanctuary moderation team through Discord.
